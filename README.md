### ALGO-000120: Polylogarithmic-overhead exact maximum flow
- **Subfield:** Graph algorithms, parallelism, and dynamic computation
- **Open:** Compute exact maximum flow in \(m\,\mathrm{polylog}(nU)\) time for polynomially encoded integral capacities bounded by \(U\).
- **Status:** Almost-linear \(m^{1+o(1)}\) algorithms are a major breakthrough, but do not automatically give a fixed polylogarithmic overhead.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would turn asymptotic near-linearity into a stronger, quantitatively controlled primitive.
- **Key works:** Chen, Kyng, Liu, Peng, Probst Gutenberg, and Sachdeva (2022), *Maximum Flow and Minimum-Cost Flow in Almost-Linear Time*, arXiv:2203.00671.
- **Related:** Operations research—transportation and circulation optimization.
